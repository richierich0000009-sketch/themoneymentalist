The Money Mentalist Autopilot app

"Money Mentalist Autopilot" is a tool used only by the owner of The Money Mentalist channel to upload the channel's own videos to its own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account, and to read the viewing statistics of those videos. It is not offered to the public.

  • It accesses only the accounts of The Money Mentalist, with the owner's authorisation.
  • It stores the access credentials for those accounts on the owner's own computer and nowhere else.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any information about viewers or other users. Viewing statistics are read in aggregate, as provided by each platform, to improve the channel's videos.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page or from the Facebook and Instagram app settings.

Use of YouTube data through this app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
